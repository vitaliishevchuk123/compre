# Compre — Curriculum Plan

## 🟢 A1 · Beginner — ≈ 500–1,000 words

Enough for very simple dialogues.

### People
boy, girl, man, woman, baby, friend, teacher

### Family
mother, father, brother, sister, son, daughter, grandma, grandpa

### Body
head, eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand, foot, arm, leg

### Animals
dog, cat, bird, fish, horse, cow, rabbit, bear

### Objects / Home
table, chair, bed, door, window, book, bag, cup, phone, car, house

### Food & Drink
water, milk, bread, apple, banana, egg, rice, soup

### Colors
red, blue, green, yellow, white, black, orange, pink

### Numbers
one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten

### Verbs (actions)
eat, drink, run, walk, jump, sleep, sit, stand, play, go, come, see,
read, write, talk, listen, open, close, give, take, like, want, have

### Adjectives
big, small, fast, slow, happy, sad, angry, hot, cold, good, bad,
old, new, clean, dirty, tall, short, long

### Adverbs & basic connectors
very, too, here, there, yes, no, please, thank you, sorry
